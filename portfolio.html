Our Heritage & Story

The Karayel Ltd Journey: Reimagining Mauritian Interiors

Founded with a vision to democratize premium furniture design in Mauritius, Karayel Ltd created the Furnish Now brand to bridge the gap between overly expensive imported European showrooms and cheap, short-lived furniture.

Operating from our central hub and showroom in Cassis, Port Louis, we cultivate direct relationships with leading international manufacturers. Every piece — from high-density foam sofa beds to multi-point ergonomic office chairs — is chosen for the demands of the Mauritian climate and lifestyle.

Our Guiding Principles
  • Transparency: Transparent pricing in Mauritian Rupees with immediate 15% VAT clarity.
  • Speed & Convenience: Reliable island-wide delivery and direct mobile payment via MCB Juice.
  • Durability: Industrial-grade gas lifts, scratch-resistant sintered slate, and reinforced hardwood structures.

Historical Milestones

2021 — Establishment of Karayel Ltd

Registered in Port Louis (BRN: C21184074). Established the Cassis facility and began supplying commercial clients with ergonomic task seating.

2023 — Launch of Furnish Now Online

Deployed the direct-to-consumer store at furnishnow.mu, pioneering local digital commerce with seamless MCB Juice checkout.

2024 — Expansion into Commercial Turnkey

Secured major corporate contracts in Ébène Cybercity and Grand Baie hospitality venues, introducing bespoke bulk procurement services.

2026 — Next-Gen Modernization

Preparation for Odoo 19 upgrade, launch of dedicated B2B portfolio portal, and implementation of new seasonal marketing design systems.
